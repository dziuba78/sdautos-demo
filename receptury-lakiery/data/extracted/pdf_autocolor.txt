COLOUR FAMILY – Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, Red, etc.
FACE – Colour viewing angle that is heavily influenced by metallic or pearl 
pigments in a formula.
FLOP – Colour viewing angle that is generally darker and influenced mostly

TONE – Cast of the colour in relation to its hue shift and adjacent colour family 
(green-shade blue, mid-shade blue, red-shade blue).
